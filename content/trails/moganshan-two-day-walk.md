This is a draft route for a slower weekend walk in Moganshan.

## Day 1

- Arrive before noon and avoid trying to compress the entire trip into one long loop.
- Walk the ridge section in the afternoon while the light is still directional.
- Leave an hour open for stopping, drawing, or doing nothing.

## Day 2

- Start early enough to catch the quieter paths.
- Take the longer descent instead of the fastest one if the weather stays dry.
- Record what the legs remember differently from what the map suggested.

The plan is intentionally loose. A trail entry should be able to hold logistics and atmosphere at the same time.
