I want a small personal scale for weather that is less about temperature and more about behavior.

Not "18C and cloudy," but:

- window-open weather
- long-walk weather
- library weather
- weather that changes how the city sounds

This might become a recurring tag system across notes and trail logs. Or it might stay a half-formed experiment. Both outcomes are acceptable.
