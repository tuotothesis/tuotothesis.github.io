title: The Value of an Unfinished Note
date: 2026-04-12

summary: A note does not need to be complete to be useful. Sometimes its value is simply that it exists.
---

An unfinished note is often closer to the actual shape of thinking than a polished essay.

When I only publish what feels complete, I hide most of the process that produced it. That makes the final result look cleaner, but it also makes the system less honest. A garden should keep some traces of uncertainty.
